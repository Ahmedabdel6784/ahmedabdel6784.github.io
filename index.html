🤔 What Is hellogrok?

hellogrok is a small but powerful helper program that runs quietly on your computer. Imagine you have a special set of AI models connected to Grok Build — but your favorite apps and tools don’t speak the same “language” as Grok Build. hellogrok acts like a friendly translator, sitting between your apps and your models. It ensures every app can talk to your custom models, no matter what format they expect.

It works on Windows, macOS, and Linux. This guide focuses on setting it up on Windows so you can start using it in minutes.

🧭 Quick Start Guide

🛠️ Troubleshooting

❓ Antivirus warning when starting hellogrok?

This can happen with any newly developed app. Click “Allow” or “Run Anyway” — hellogrok is safe and doesn’t collect your data. If blocked, you can whitelist the folder in your antivirus settings.

❓ Apps say “Connection refused”?

Make sure hellogrok is actually running (look for the small window). If you closed it, double-click the hellogrok.exe again. Also check your firewall permission popup when first launching.

❓ Port 8080 already used?

hellogrok detects this automatically and switches to another free port. Update your app’s API URL to match the displayed port (e.g., http://localhost:8081).
